About Me

I have focused my research on developing calibration frameworks for low-cost particulate matter sensors, addressing how aerosol composition, hygroscopic growth, and instrument optics drive sensor bias

I see aerosols from the lens of a physicist, and that is what I reach for first when aerosols behave strangely. I am now looking toward a research career in academia

Thank you for taking the time to visit and learn about my work
