About Me

I work on the measurement of airborne particulate matter — specifically, on making inexpensive optical sensors accurate enough that the data they produce can be trusted. My doctoral work spanned controlled chamber experiments through to multi-site field deployments across Delhi–NCR and the Indo-Gangetic Plain.

I came to aerosols from physics, and that is still what I reach for first when a sensor behaves strangely. I am now looking toward a research career in academia.

Thank you for taking the time to visit and learn about my work.
