Vasudev Malyan

Air Quality • Aerosol Science • Sensor Technologies

I am a Technical Expert in Air Quality Sensor Technologies at Ubiqedge Technologies Private Limited, leading the development of network-scale calibration models and source characterisation approaches for ongoing low-cost PM sensor deployments in Mumbai.

I completed my Ph.D. in the Environmental Science and Engineering Department at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, supervised by Prof. Manoranjan Sahu and supported by the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship. My work combines physics-based modelling and machine learning to make inexpensive particulate matter sensors accurate enough that the data they produce can be relied upon — from controlled chamber experiments through to multi-site field deployments across Delhi–NCR and the Indo-Gangetic Plain.
