Research & Impact

While the proliferation of low-cost air quality sensors has transformed environmental monitoring, their raw outputs are skewed by particle composition, size, and humidity. I bridge the gap between raw data and reality.

By building robust physical models and advanced calibration techniques, I make low-cost particulate matter monitoring trustworthy. From controlled chamber experiments to city-scale networks, my work ensures that environmental data can drive evidence-based decisions on public health and policy.

I am currently seeking postdoctoral positions in aerosol science, air quality measurement and atmospheric instrumentation. Do get in touch if there is a fit.
