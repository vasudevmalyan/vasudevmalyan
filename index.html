Vasudev Malyan[cite: 1]

I am a Technical Expert in Air Quality Sensor Technologies at Ubiqedge Technologies Private Limited, leading the development of network-scale calibration models and source characterization for low-cost PM sensor deployments[cite: 1].

I earned my Ph.D. from the Environmental Science and Engineering Department at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, where my research focused on the calibration of low-cost particulate matter sensors using physics-based and machine learning frameworks[cite: 1].
