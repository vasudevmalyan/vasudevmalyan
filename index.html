What I work on

Cheap air quality sensors are now everywhere, but the numbers they report are often wrong in ways that are easy to miss: they respond to what particles are made of, how large they are and how humid the air is, not to mass alone. I build the physics and the models that make those readings trustworthy — in a controlled chamber, across city-scale networks, and from optical first principles — so that dense, low-cost monitoring can support real decisions about exposure and policy.

I am currently seeking postdoctoral positions in aerosol science, air quality measurement and atmospheric instrumentation. Do get in touch if there is a fit.
