Vasudev Malyan

I am a Technical Expert in Air Quality Sensor Technologies at Ubiqedge Technologies Private Limited, leading the development of network-scale calibration models and source characterization approaches for ongoing low-cost PM sensor deployments.

Having earned my Ph.D. from the Environmental Science and Engineering Department at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, my work integrates expertise in physics-based modeling and machine learning frameworks to develop evidence-based insights supporting air quality management and environmental monitoring.
