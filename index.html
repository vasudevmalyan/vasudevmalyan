Vasudev Malyan | Researcher Vasudev Malyan Environmental Science • PM2.5 • Sensors About Research Contact Vasudev Malyan I am a researcher at the Environmental Science and Engineering Department at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. Working within the Aerosol & Nanoparticle Technology Laboratory, my work integrates environmental modeling and instrumentation to improve our understanding of air quality. Specifically, I focus on evaluating, modeling, and calibrating low-cost sensors for measuring and analyzing fine particulate matter (PM2.5). My goal is to develop reliable, data-driven approaches that can support effective air quality management and policy decisions.
