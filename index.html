Vasudev Malyan

Technical Expert in Air Quality Sensor Technologies at Ubiqedge Technologies Private Limited. I lead the development of network-scale calibration models and source characterization approaches for low-cost PM sensor deployments.

I earned my Ph.D. from the Environmental Science and Engineering Department at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. My research integrates expertise in physics-based modeling and machine learning frameworks to advance environmental monitoring and policy decision-making.
